Gearlift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Gearlift ("the app", "we") handles information when you use it.

No account required

Gearlift does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use it. There is no login, no username, no email collection, no password.

Data stored on your device

Gearlift stores the following information locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage:

  • Your equipment, muscle, and training preference selections
  • Your workout history (which exercises you did, when, and your progress through them), including any names you give your sessions

None of this data is transmitted to us, to any server we operate, or to any third party. We cannot see it, access it, or recover it — it exists only on your device. If you uninstall the app, clear its storage, or lose your device without a backup, this data is gone permanently; there is no cloud account to recover it from. This is a deliberate trade-off of not requiring an account.

Exercise content and images

Exercise names, instructions, and photos are drawn from an open, public-domain exercise dataset. Exercise photos are loaded over the internet from a content delivery network (jsDelivr, serving files from a GitHub repository we maintain) rather than stored in the app itself. Loading an image involves your device making a standard web request to that service, which may log technical information common to any web request (such as your IP address and the file requested) under its own privacy practices — we do not receive or control this information.

Analytics

Gearlift uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used — for example, which screens are opened and how often, and to detect crashes. This data is aggregated and is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal identifier, because we don't collect those in the first place. Firebase Analytics is provided by Google; see Google's Privacy Policy for how Google itself handles this data.

Children's privacy

Gearlift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, this applies equally to all users.

No ads, no data sales

Gearlift does not display advertising and does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds accounts, cloud sync, or new data collection — we'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
